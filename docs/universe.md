# Stock universe (20 tickers)

Locked: 4 stocks per sector across 5 sectors. All large-cap, liquid, with full 5-year price history and reliable FMP fundamentals coverage.

| Sector | Tickers |
|---|---|
| Technology | AAPL, MSFT, GOOGL, NVDA |
| Financials | JPM, BAC, GS, AXP |
| Healthcare | JNJ, UNH, PFE, LLY |
| Industrials | BA, CAT, HON, GE |
| Consumer | AMZN, WMT, KO, NKE |

Selection criteria:
1. Market cap > $50B at the start of the date range (2021-01-01)
2. Listed on NYSE or NASDAQ for the full 5 years
3. FMP Starter tier covers their quarterly fundamentals
4. 10-K filings available on SEC EDGAR for all 5 fiscal years
5. Non-trivial 8-K event frequency (at least 5 events in last 12 months)

The 20-stock universe was chosen to balance methodological richness (5 sectors enables cross-sectional analysis) against project scope (small enough that every row is inspectable manually).
